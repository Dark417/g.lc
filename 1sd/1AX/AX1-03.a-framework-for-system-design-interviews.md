# Volume 1, Chapter 3: A Framework For System Design Interviews

This chapter is the conceptual frame. [`../0flow.md`](../0flow.md) is the repository's current executable version, and [`../build.md`](../build.md) is the written-answer contract.

## Four-step frame

1. **Understand and bound:** restate the problem, propose scope, rank numeric NFRs, and name non-goals.
2. **Propose high-level design:** define APIs and data model, draw the system, and trace one request end to end.
3. **Deep dive:** choose the mechanism with the highest correctness or scale risk; explain state, keys, concurrency, and failure.
4. **Scale and close:** address bottlenecks, reliability, operations, tradeoffs, and the first 10× change.

## What makes the frame defensible

- Requirements select the design; the design does not retroactively invent requirements.
- Estimation must force a choice.
- The diagram includes data ownership and request direction.
- The deep dive reaches algorithm/data-structure depth.
- Every important choice has a cost and flip condition.
- Failure handling includes degraded behavior and recovery, not just redundancy.
